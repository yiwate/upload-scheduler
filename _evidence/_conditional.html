Conditional evidence — Personal Upload Scheduler

Google Cloud project 49718646075 (yoake-archives-uploader) · Use case: video uploading and account management · OAuth 2.0: yes

1. OAuth 2.0 scopes requested by the client

Declared in _pipeline/youtube_uploader.py. These are the only scopes the client ever requests. Access can be revoked by the operator at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

SCOPES = [
    "https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload",
    "https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube",
    "https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl",
]

The client first verifies that the OAuth token belongs to the expected channel, then uploads the video file and sets the custom thumbnail.
