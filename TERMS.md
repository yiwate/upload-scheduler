## 3. Permitted use

The tool may be used only to:

- upload video files that the operator created and owns;
- set the title, description, tags, category, thumbnail and scheduled publish time of

- read back those same videos to confirm an upload succeeded;
- add those videos to playlists on the operator's own channels.

The tool must not be used to access, collect, store or process data belonging to any
other YouTube user or channel.

## 4. Prohibited use

The tool must not be used to:

- scrape, cache, aggregate or republish other people's YouTube content or metadata;
- circumvent, disable or interfere with any part of YouTube API Services;
- upload content the operator does not have the rights to publish;
- misrepresent the nature of uploaded content, including the "made for kids" and
  "altered or synthetic content" declarations.

## 5. Content declarations

Every upload sets the "made for kids" and "altered or synthetic content" flags according
to the actual nature of the video. Videos containing AI-generated voice or imagery are
declared as synthetic media and state this in the video description.
